How it works

  1. Prepare
    Each service lists the documents and the exact fee before you start.
  1. Apply and pay
    Your answers are saved as you go. Pay with MoMo, Airtel Money, card or USSD.
  1. Follow your request
    One number to keep: your application number. We send SMS updates.

🔒 Your data is safe with us

Only the institution that handles your request (for example DGIE for visas) can see your information. Irembo staff do not read your documents. Every form explains why each personal question is asked.
