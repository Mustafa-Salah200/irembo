Renew my visa

Extend your stay in Rwanda before your current visa ends. Your request is handled by DGIE, the Directorate General of Immigration and Emigration.

Time to apply
About 10 min
Decision
~4 working days
Fee
20,000 – 50,000 RWF

Prepare these before you start

Tick them off as you gather them.

How much does it cost?

You pay only after you submit, and you see the exact amount first. Sample fees for this concept: the real fees must come from DGIE.

Your data and privacy

Who sees your information? Only DGIE officers who process visa requests. Irembo staff do not read your documents.

Why are personal details like parents' names needed?

They help DGIE match your request to your immigration record and prevent identity fraud.

How long is it kept?

As long as the law requires for immigration records. You can ask DGIE for a copy of your data.

Proposed wording. The final text must follow the official DGIE / Irembo privacy policy.

Questions people ask

I am a foreigner. Do I need a Rwandan National ID?

No. Your passport number is enough.

What if my payment code expires?

Open your application and press "Get a new payment code". You don't need to apply again.

Can someone apply for me?

Yes. An Irembo agent can apply with you. Bring your documents. Find an agent.
