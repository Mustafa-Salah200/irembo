Renew my visa

Extend your stay in Rwanda before your current visa ends. Your request is handled by DGIE (Directorate General of Immigration and Emigration).

Time to applyabout 10 minutes
Decisionusually 4 working days
Fee20,000 – 50,000 RWF

Prepare these before you start

  • Passport biodata page (the page with your photo) PDF file · 500 KB or smaller
  • A recent passport photo JPG file · 200 KB or smaller · square, at least 400 × 400 pixels · plain light background.
    Too big? Don't worry: we can make it smaller for you.
  • Your current visa number Printed on your visa sticker or e-visa letter
  • A phone number for MTN MoMo or Airtel Money, or a bank card To pay the fee and receive SMS updates

🔒 Your data and privacy

Who sees your information? Only DGIE officers who process visa requests. Irembo staff do not read your documents.

Why are personal details like parents' names needed?DGIE uses them to match your request to your immigration record and to prevent identity fraud.
How long is it kept?For as long as Rwandan law on immigration records requires. You can ask DGIE for a copy of your data.

Questions people ask

I am a foreigner. Do I need a Rwandan National ID?No. You apply with your passport or travel document.
What if my payment code expires?Your application is kept. Open it and press "Get a new payment code". You do not need to apply again.
Can someone apply for me?Yes. An Irembo agent can apply with you. Bring the documents listed above. Find an agent.
