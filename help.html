Common questions

My photo is rejected. What do I do?The upload page shows which rule the photo breaks (file type, size or shape). If it is a JPG, press "Make it fit for me": we cut it to a square and make it smaller. Your face is not changed.
My payment code (Bill ID) expired. Do I need to apply again?No. Open your application (from My applications or Find application) and press "Get a new payment code". Your answers and documents are kept.
Which number do I need to keep?Only your application number (it starts with B2). The payment code is linked to it. If you lose it, search with your phone number.
I am not Rwandan. Do I need a National ID?No. Foreign residents apply with a passport or travel document.
Someone asked me for my mobile-money PIN. Is that normal?No. Irembo and government staff will never ask for your PIN. Only type it on your own phone when you approve a payment. Report it by calling 9099.
Can I stop and continue later?Yes. Your answers are saved as you type. Come back from My applications.

🔒 Privacy: how your data is used

Who sees it? Only the institution that handles your request. For visas, that is DGIE officers. Irembo staff do not read your documents.

Why do we ask personal questions? Each form explains why. For example, parents' names help DGIE match your request to your immigration record and prevent identity fraud.

How long is it kept? As long as the law for that type of record requires. You can ask the institution for a copy of your data.

Payments: Card details go directly to your bank. Mobile-money payments are approved with your PIN on your own phone. Nobody else needs your PIN.
