Privacy: how your data is used

Who sees it?

Only the institution handling your request. For visas, that is DGIE officers. Irembo staff do not read your documents.

Why personal questions?

Each form explains why. Parents' names help DGIE match your immigration record and prevent fraud.

How long is it kept?

As long as the law requires for that record. You can ask the institution for a copy.

Payments

Card details go to your bank. Mobile money is approved with your PIN on your own phone. Nobody else needs it.
